Our commitment

WhittWorks Studios aims to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at level AA. This site is meant to be usable by as many people as possible, including people who use a keyboard, a screen reader or other assistive tools.

What has been done

Every part of the site can be used from the keyboard, and the focus indicator is visible as you move through it.

Text contrast was checked against the AA requirement.

The site respects your device's reduced-motion setting.

There is a text-first version of the site for phones and for anyone without JavaScript. It has the same content as the cork board.

Form fields have labels, and error messages are announced to screen readers.

A known limitation

On a wide screen, the cork board shows the sections as papers pinned to a board. The papers themselves are mouse shortcuts. The keyboard path to every section is the menu button at the top right of the page. Pressing the Escape key returns to the whole board.

Reporting a problem

If something on this site is hard to use, please email caden@whittworkstudios.com. Tell us what page you were on and what got in your way. We will reply within five business days.
